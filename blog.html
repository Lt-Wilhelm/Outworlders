Welcome to Outworlders!

November 24, 2025

Welcome to the official Outworlders blog! This is where I'll be sharing updates about the comic, behind-the-scenes content, and my thoughts on the creative process.

First, this is a comic inspired by Rich Burlew's Order of the Stick, a world that I've been a fan of since early highschool. I actually tried to do a comic in the style of OOTS back then, but it took till this year to actually get everything together and give it a fair try. I figure writing out a sci-fi manuscript in the meantime helped.

Second, the journey of creating Outworlders has been quite the fun ride so far. From a visit to Japan inspiring me to write one of the protagonists, to pivoting from the written to a visual medium, there has been a lot that has gone into this ongoing learning experience.

Stay tuned for regular updates as new pages are released. Thanks for being part of this adventure!
